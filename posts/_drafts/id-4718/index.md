And if you ever have been interested on emergent behaviours, then you probably may have heard about cellular automata.

Cellular automata are minimalistic constructions in which individual agents (that we refer to as cells) can take an internal state given internal rules. At any given moment in time, the rules take into consideration the current state of the cell and the states of the surrounding cells, and then a new internal state is calculated.

The cells have always a starting state, and are arranged given a neighbourhood function (some kind of tiled pattern on its simplest form). The neighbourhood itself is not meant to change, only the state of the cell at different time steps.

Different combinations of stating states, rules and neighbouhoods create different behaviours.
