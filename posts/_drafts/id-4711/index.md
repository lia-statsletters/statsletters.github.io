From a previous blogpost you may recall us using confidence intervals of the Binomial distribution to establish a sample size to estate how much errors you could expect on a dataset.

A big assumption there was that all parts of your dataset had to have the same probability of failure. But what happens when that assumption simply does not hold? This can be the case if your dataset was built under different conditions e.g. equipment of different quality, different annotation companies, or crowdsourced data.
